# Interview Prep Notes — PwC Director, Government & Public Sector (G&PS) Practice

## 1. Role Context: What PwC Looks For

PwC's G&PS Director is a **market-facing leadership role** — not a delivery role, not a pure sales role, but a hybrid. You own business growth, client relationships, and customer success across the public sector portfolio. Think "Client Partner" with P&L accountability.

**What the hiring manager cares about:**
- Can you **originate** new business — not just receive RFPs and respond?
- Do you have **existing senior government relationships** you can bring?
- Can you **oversee delivery outcomes** without micromanaging — "outcomes not execution"?
- Can you build and lead **ecosystem partnerships** (SIs, tech partners)?
- Do you understand PwC's consulting model — advisory + implementation + managed services?

## 3. Likely Interview Questions & How to Answer

### "How do you originate business — not just respond to RFPs?"
**Answer framework:** Walk through your pre-engagement methodology — identify transformation priorities through relationship building, shape the client's thinking before procurement begins, and position yourself as a strategic advisor. Reference how MTI started as a relationship, not an RFP.

### "PwC is a consulting firm, not a product company. How does your experience translate?"
**Answer framework:** Most of your career has been consulting-led — VISEO (consulting + Salesforce implementation), NCS (pure consulting/SI), Capgemini (consulting), Logica (consulting). Even at Workato, you sold services-wrapped engagements, not just licences. You understand the consulting model: advisory → implementation → managed services → repeat business.

### "How do you ensure customer success across engagements?"
**Answer framework:** You own the relationship end-to-end. At VISEO, you led pilot-to-production cycles ensuring outcomes. At NCS, you provided executive oversight across concurrent programmes. At Workato, you partner with delivery teams and act as escalation point. The pattern: you stay close enough to catch risks early, but let delivery teams execute.

### "Tell me about a time you managed a delivery failure or escalation"
**Prepare this.** Pick a real example where a programme hit trouble and you stepped in. Show: (1) you caught it early through client proximity, (2) you escalated appropriately, (3) you worked with delivery to remediate, (4) the client relationship was preserved or strengthened.

### "How do you build a go-to-market strategy for government?"
**Answer framework:** Data-led territory mapping (which agencies are spending on what), relationship cultivation before there's a deal, multi-agency coverage strategy (MTI Family approach), and co-sell with ecosystem partners. Reference the >300% pipeline expansion.

### "What's your approach to ecosystem partnerships?"
**Answer framework:** You've built co-sell strategies with Salesforce, MuleSoft, Workato, CISCO, and ST Engineering. The key is identifying where the partner's capability complements PwC's advisory strength — not competing, but extending. Reference ARM + CISCO/ST Eng on Smart Nation.

### "How do you balance sales leadership with delivery oversight?"
**Answer framework:** These aren't separate — they're a flywheel. Delivery success drives repeat business. You stay close enough to client sentiment to catch risks early, but you don't manage individual workstreams. At VISEO, this model delivered 200–400% quota attainment because every successful delivery generated the next deal.
